Drone Capstone Notes

Feasibility
- Can build something that kinda flies, sensors will be the hard part
- Existing broken parts not really useful, would still need to build from scratch

Regulations
- Federal regulation restricts import/sale/commercialization of products with foreign made parts
- FCC regulation on advanced ground robotics coming, similar to drone rules
- For parts, look at NDAA compliance (no parts made in China)
- No existing tag/label that shows a part has no foreign made components

Flying on campus
- There is a process for flying drones at UCM, need to fill out a form
- No flying over people
- Test flights on campus do not need a drone license
- Need online safety training, takes a few hours

- Look into ArcGIS
